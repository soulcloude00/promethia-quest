How scoring works

Total /100 = Problem-solving 35 (10 puzzle islands: Color Stones, Gearbot, Crate Crush, Story Stones, Speaker's Stage, Shadow Golem, Robot Sorter, Steady Voice, Mirror Twins, Fix the Robot; full marks for solving, −10% per failed try, −12% per hint, min 40% if solved) + Persistence 20 (islands finished + coming back after a fail) + Curiosity 15 (secret stars, cracked walls, hidden bushes) + Creativity 20 (build challenge accuracy 8 + speed 2, bonus free build/naming 4, Dream Machine invention 3, Tale Trail story 3) + Communication 10 (hello line 3, steady pace 2, build reason 1.5, story ending 1.5, invention line 1, boss reflection 1). Badges: each student gets their top 5 out of 20, earned from what they actually did. Time is recorded but not scored.
