How scoring works

Total /100 = Problem-solving 35 (6 puzzle levels; full marks for solving, −10% per failed try, −12% per hint, min 40% if solved) + Persistence 20 (levels finished + coming back after a fail) + Curiosity 15 (secret stars, cracked walls, hidden bushes) + Creativity 20 (free build: blocks, materials, height, spread, naming) + Communication 10 (typed hello line, build reason, boss reflection). Time is recorded but not scored.
